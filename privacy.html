Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

L&A Custom Homes uses L&A Construction Manager to coordinate projects, employee access, time-clock activity, and related construction operations.

Information we collect

We may collect employee or contractor names, work contact information, account and sign-in activity, project assignments, reports, time records, and location submitted during check-in or check-out. Location is recorded only when the user starts a check-in or check-out action.

SMS information

Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are collected through our public, voluntary SMS consent form and are used only for transactional messages such as one-time sign-in codes, employee or contractor account invitations, and essential account or time-clock notifications. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent records are not sold, rented, or shared for third-party marketing.

How we use and protect information

Information is used to operate the construction-management service, secure accounts, support employees and contractors, maintain business records, and comply with legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and limit access to authorized personnel and service providers that help operate the application.

Choices and contact

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop SMS messages or HELP for help. Opting out of SMS may prevent phone-based sign-in. For privacy questions, contact L&A Custom Homes at (650) 374-4572.
